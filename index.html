Built for the stands and the bench

No complicated setup. Open the app, pick your teams and tap the ice.

🎯

One gesture per event

Tap for a shot, double tap for a goal, drag for missed and blocked shots, icings, offsides, turnovers and faceoffs, long press for a hit. The score updates live.

↩️

Fix anything, anytime

Browse every event on a timeline: edit it, delete it or drag it to the right spot on the ice. No more “undo, undo, undo”.

⏱️

Just like a real game

Teams switch ends every period, NHL-style overtime and a shootout when needed. The app asks if the game is over at the right time.

📋

Instant game summary

A scoreboard with the team logos, the scoring summary and head-to-head game stats by period, plus a rink map of every shot. Share a PDF report in seconds.

📸

Images for social media

Express (the score and the rink) or Detailed (with charts): copy the image and paste it in Facebook, Instagram or Messenger.

🏆

Leagues and tournaments

Group your games by league season or tournament, with a record for each. Mark playoff and exhibition games.

📈

Season analysis

Trends game after game, home and road splits, a season shot map and head-to-head records against every opponent.

🏒

Clubs and teams

Organize teams by club, category and division, with their own logo, color and abbreviation.

🔒

Private and bilingual

No account, no tracking, no ads. Everything stays on your iPhone, in English or French, with a full backup you can export anytime.
